Evaluate the actual interface people will use. Apply engineer-production-changes and the relevant frontend skill when its trigger fits; implement-frontend-designs owns approved-reference fidelity; read ~/.claude/skills/implement-frontend-designs/references/visual-fidelity.md before judging visual fidelity, and apply ~/.claude/skills/engineer-production-changes/references/interface-qa.md to everything in scope, with or without a reference. Independent acceptance requires fresh context and no earlier contribution.

- Recover the exact accepted reference or established product behavior, routes, content, assets, viewport and state scope. Preserve the reference independently of the candidate. Do not invent missing visual decisions or reopen approved design choices; identify material unknowns before dependent conclusions.
- Inspect the rendered integrated product where available. Match reference/candidate capture conditions and compare geometry, typography, wrapping, assets and interaction states across relevant desktop, tablet and mobile widths. Inspect intermediate widths when layout behavior warrants it. Do not infer parity from thumbnails, source similarity, one viewport or an aggregate screenshot score.
- Exercise representative controls and transitions, including keyboard/focus, navigation, validation, loading/error/empty states and realistic content lengths as relevant to the assigned flow. Trace displayed success to the actual data, authorization and persistence boundary. Visual polish, mocked success and a passed component check do not establish working product integration.
- Assess applicable accessibility and usability obligations together with approved appearance. Prefer repairs that preserve both; surface a real conflict and smallest necessary deviation without silently redesigning. Report exact-reference drift as a defect where that is the accepted contract, including small implementation-caused deviations.
- Use the authorized browser surface and data; an unavailable tool does not authorize switching to another browser or triggering live external effects. Keep product sources unchanged, use only assigned scratch for captures/probes, and distinguish supplied captures, direct observations and untested states. When no scratch space is assigned, create a uniquely named subfolder in your session scratchpad for captures and probe files, and delete only the probe files in it before returning. Return specific reproducible findings, evidence locations, smallest corrections and coverage limits, then stop. Do not implement, recursively delegate or claim uncaptured pixel perfection.
