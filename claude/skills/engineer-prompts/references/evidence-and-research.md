# Evidence and research

Use when external facts or target-specific behavior affect the prompt. Model memory supplies leads,
not evidence. Keep material choices traceable to inspected sources, target/version, date and limits
as relevant.

## Verify the target before tailoring

For a specified target, inspect current official prompting guidance and documentation for its
model/version, product, modality and interface, including relevant migration notes and exceptions.
Prefer exact-target guidance over broader provider advice. Adequate current supplied official
material can suffice; respect browsing restrictions and research accessible facts before asking.

Verify the mechanics the prompt uses:

- message hierarchy, prompt placement, instruction language and supported input modalities;
- reference/attachment syntax, context limits and retrieval or state capabilities;
- structured output, schema support, tools, result/error semantics and execution ownership;
- external parameters, reasoning modes and supported or incompatible combinations;
- prompting recommendations and documented version-specific differences.

Apply guidance for its documented or measured contribution to this outcome. Provider examples do
not authorize features or changed constraints; example counts, tags, reasoning phrases and
self-check routines are not universal. One target's recommendations do not establish another's
performance. Resolve conflicts with the task using evidence and authorized choices.

## Match evidence to the claim

- Official documentation supports documented capabilities, syntax, policies and recommendations.
  Inspect the supporting text; URLs, snippets, titles and another model's account do not suffice.
- A direct authorized run supports the observed behavior for its recorded prompt, target,
  interface, settings, inputs and date. It does not establish universal behavior.
- The user's designated source governs their own policies, requirements and processes. Supplied
  data and reports establish what was supplied, not independent verification of external assertions.
- Other material external, domain, version-, environment- or data-dependent premises need current
  claim-appropriate primary records, observations or authoritative documentation. Use reputable
  analysis where relevant; community reports are attributed reports or leads, not broader proof.

Label inferences and hypotheses with traceable inputs. Attribution or hedging does not make an
unverified premise usable as a steering instruction. Keep evidence notes outside the target prompt
unless it needs them.

## Resolve missing or conflicting support

Compare authority, target/interface scope, dates, versions and directness. Do not average
incompatible recommendations or choose convenient facts. Distinguish documented-support conflicts
from limited observations. Use a focused authorized test when useful; otherwise state the uncertainty
and its effect.

If bounded useful research cannot establish a required premise, obtain the source or decision,
omit only optional dependent behavior, use an intentional non-steering runtime input, or block
the dependent result. Propose hypotheses for testing; never encode them as verified facts.

When exact-target guidance is unavailable, identify the gap. With resolved intent, offer a labeled
target-neutral draft and its remaining adaptation/evaluation needs when useful; do not claim target
optimization. Deliberately model-agnostic work needs no invented provider research, but material
task premises still need evidence. Neither path resolves user policy, schema, context or authority.

Use the runtime bindings in [prompt-contract.md](prompt-contract.md) for changing mechanics, policies and data. Do not
imply the target can browse or update itself without actual capability and authority.

## Protect private context

Research public mechanics with generic public or redacted queries. Disclose private inputs,
proprietary schemas, identifiers, documents or credentials only when necessary and authorized for
that destination. Same-task reviewers are inside the boundary only under the same data controls;
onward services require separate authority. Apply the authority process in [prompt-contract.md](prompt-contract.md) and
use adequate nondisclosing alternatives where possible.
