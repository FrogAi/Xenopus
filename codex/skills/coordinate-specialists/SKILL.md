# Coordinate Specialists

The primary agent owns the outcome, orchestration, integration and completion claim. Delegate every materially useful distinct contribution; keep coordination internal unless sharing it helps the task.

## Select and assign work

Derive bounded deliverables from the requested outcome, requirements, evidence, constraints and completion criteria. Identify dependencies and reassess as the work changes. Follow applicable domain skills and their phase/ownership contracts rather than starting competing workflows. A delegated worker stays within its parent's remit and capacity plan. Do not manufacture work, run a fixed roster or turn a simple task into an orchestration exercise.

Choose the contribution by the unresolved decision: establish a cause, decide what would prove behavior, challenge a design or implementation, or examine a domain risk such as performance, security, frontend behavior or operational state. Use bounded evidence-preparation or execution roles for their assigned subquestions; keep unresolved judgments with the primary or a suitable judgment role. Assign investigation or design challenge before dependent implementation when its result can change that work; select only useful contributions and reuse equivalent coverage.

Discover the current agent catalog through supported runtime capabilities or configured profile sources. Inspect relevant definitions and select by the actual missing method or deliverable, not names or assumed expertise. Verify selection and availability again when they change.

- Use a fitting saved profile through a verified supported dispatch mechanism and honor its installed model/reasoning preset unless a verified maintenance change replaces it.
- If no profile fits or dispatch is unavailable, brief a general agent. Where authorized and supported, pass a relevant profile's method and preset explicitly. Report material mismatches: a task name or self-report does not prove loading, and supplied instructions cannot enforce missing permissions or tool controls. Do not do work requiring unavailable enforcement.
- A missing profile must not suppress useful delegation. If no worthwhile contribution exists or tools/capacity are unavailable, use an adequate direct or sequential fallback and disclose material coverage gaps.

Give each agent its objective, relevant requirements and evidence, exact question or owned targets, dependencies, action/data boundaries, and stopping condition. Supply only necessary context while preserving evidence access. Require a concise result with auditable support, actual changes or actions, checks, gaps and integration needs.

Assign one active owner per mutable target or external action. Exploration and review leave targets unchanged; explicitly assign isolated probe artifacts when needed. Parallel writers require isolated targets and a clear integration path. Settle the previous owner's actual state before transferring ownership.

Run independent work in parallel while advancing different useful work yourself. Sequence dependencies, track assignments with the existing task mechanism, and reserve capacity for fresh audits. Keep nested delegation within the same remit and ownership plan. On failure, nonresponse or exhausted capacity, use bounded waits, preserve completed evidence, settle active owners and take the best adequate fallback; do not queue indefinitely.

When waiting is the only useful next step, prefer the runtime's event-driven wait over repeated status snapshots or transcript reads. Choose supported wait and wrapper-yield durations that avoid needless model wakeups while honoring responsiveness requirements. Retain handles and result cursors, consume new results, and lengthen unchanged waits where allowed. A timeout alone does not establish failure or justify restarting work; inspect status when it can change the next action.

For understood code changes, implement directly unless the piece is independent of other work in progress or its output would flood the primary agent's context; then use a focused implementation worker while the primary agent owns design, acceptance and integration. Assign the smallest coherent, verifiable behavior, with narrow write ownership and enough surrounding context to preserve its contracts; never divide by line count. Choose a lighter preset only when it fits the reasoning and risk; use stronger or direct work when uncertainty, consequences, underperformance or coordination overhead warrants it. Before releasing dependent work, inspect the actual changes and relevant check results against the engineering requirements, challenge the necessity of the design itself, and resolve supported findings. Independent work may proceed in parallel under the ownership rules above. Keep these checkpoints internal unless the user requested approval between pieces. The primary agent's acceptance of its own plan does not replace the fresh independent final review required by the engineering skill.

For incidents, coordinate distinct evidence questions and assign authorized mitigation to one owner. Verify the affected service flow; justified mitigation need not await full root-cause analysis. Finish the requested investigation without expanding it into unbounded maintenance.

## Audit, reconcile and finish

Before consequential, complex or broad completion, obtain each distinct independent audit with material expected value. Passing primary-agent checks do not replace useful independent scrutiny; equivalent independent coverage can be reused. Derive coverage from the requirements, candidate and domain risks, including correctness, regressions, causal ownership, unnecessary complexity, factual support and verification.

Start independent auditors with fresh/no-history context and no earlier participation. Provide neutral requirements, relevant baseline, an identified stable candidate and authorized evidence access, without advocacy or earlier reviewers' conclusions. Keep reviewed targets stable through snapshots, isolation or paused writes. A contributor renamed as an auditor is not independent.

Ask for disconfirming evidence and material defects, allowing challenge to the whole approach. Require every supported in-scope issue, including smaller defects, without a finding quota or arbitrary cutoff. Each finding needs a location or claim, violated requirement, triggering conditions, consequence and support. Distinguish missing evidence, unproven concerns and preferences from established defects. Full audits must account for every applicable core and domain area.

Inspect returned support and directly recheck consequential, conflicting or incomplete claims. Resolve disagreements through evidence, not votes. Give findings a supported disposition: fixed and checked, rejected with counterevidence, already satisfied/duplicate, outside authorized change scope, or unresolved with a stated gap. Do not use severity or deferral to waive required behavior. Use fresh focused adjudication when material disagreement warrants it.

Integrate and check the affected end-to-end behavior. After repairs, recheck the original counterexample and affected regressions; candidate changes invalidate affected review evidence. Prior auditors can verify repairs, but materially changed work may warrant a new independent reviewer. Implementers still perform useful local checks.

Before completion, reconcile every material assignment or provide an adequate bounded fallback, settle ownership and required fixes, and meet the task's completion checks. Report material gaps accurately. Stop when the criteria hold; a clean audit supports only its inspected scope.

## Improve the specialist library

Assess reusable opportunities when selecting expertise, when results reveal a useful method or gap, and before substantive completion. When evidence warrants creating, changing, consolidating or retiring a role, or reviewing a model preset, read [library-maintenance.md](references/library-maintenance.md). It owns admission and preset comparisons; `$improve-personal-customizations` owns verification, installation and recovery. Routine delegation with no evidenced library issue does not require that reference.
