# Investigate technical changes

Use `$engineer-production-changes` for technical investigations. Follow relevant callers, unchanged surrounding logic, defaults, configuration, hardware paths, saved-state migration and downstream limits until the claimed effect is explained. A rewrite or large diff is a reason to investigate behavior, not evidence of improvement. Changed copy, comments, tests or intentions alone do not establish product behavior; when the product is a fork, upstream behavior may not apply to it or its configuration.

Decide what readers need to understand and what comparison would establish it before drafting the claim. Inspect existing evidence first; when a material comparison is missing and feasible within the task, perform it using the relevant method: execute old and new logic on identical inputs, replay representative data, benchmark the relevant workload, or capture comparable product states and media.

Choose representative scenarios, affected profiles and meaningful limits, including cases where the benefit disappears or a tradeoff appears. Separate a setting's isolated effect from the complete path that constrains it. For adaptive behavior, examine startup, learning over time, retained state and resets. For performance or media quality, compare the relevant device, workload, settings and output; record repeatability and uncertainty where they affect the conclusion. A higher target, bitrate or resolution is not itself proof of a better experienced result. Retain inputs, versions, methods and results sufficient to reproduce material comparisons.
